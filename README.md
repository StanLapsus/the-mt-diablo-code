# The Mt. Diablo Code

**An investigation of the Zodiac Killer's 32-symbol cipher and Mt. Diablo map**

📄 **Read the report:** [The_Mt_Diablo_Code_Investigation.pdf](The_Mt_Diablo_Code_Investigation.pdf) (23 pages, illustrated)

---

## The mystery

On 26 June 1970 the man who called himself the Zodiac mailed the San Francisco Chronicle a Phillips 66 road map with a dial drawn over Mt. Diablo, and 32 cipher symbols (the "Z32"). He wrote:

> "The Map coupled with this code will tell you where the bomb is set. You have untill next Fall to dig it up."

A month later he added: *"The Mt. Diablo Code concerns Radians & # inches along the radians."*

The FBI Laboratory reported in 1970 that "no decryption could be effected". Z32 has never been solved.

## What this investigation does

Instead of guessing at the 32 symbols, it takes his written instructions literally and measures where they lead:

- It sets his dial's zero to **magnetic north**, as he instructs ("0 is to be set to Mag. N."), using the declinations printed on 1967–70 maps.
- It reads "radians" in two ways: the **mathematical radian** (57.3°) and the navigator's **radial** (the dial's clock lines).
- It computes exact geodesic bearings and distances from the NGS survey mark on the Mt. Diablo summit to his crime scenes.
- It checks the mailed map itself. Its true scale is about 7.06 mi/in, not the printed 6.4, and the sheet is rotated about 5°.
- It compares his two **bomb drawings** with 1968 USGS maps and current **Google Earth** imagery of the places the dial points to.
- It tests how far the 32 symbols can actually confirm any proposed text.
- It states the chance of coincidence for every pattern it reports.

## Key findings

| Finding | Strength |
|---|---|
| "**One radian, three inches**" from Mt. Diablo, set to magnetic north, ends **215–391 m** from the Lake Herman Road turnout, where his first two victims were killed (20 Dec 1968). | Striking; about 2–6% chance of coincidence |
| Blue Rock Springs and Washington & Cherry, two of his attack sites, are **60.06° apart** as seen from the summit: two clock hours to within 0.06°. | About 1 in 200 |
| **Clock line 10 at 3½ inches** lands on Columbus Parkway at its junction with Lake Herman Road, where the roads of his first two attacks meet. It is the only nearby stretch whose layout matches his sunlight-triggered bomb drawing: a north–south road, rising ground to the west, and the morning sun across the road. | Plausible; low confidence |
| The magnetic compass rose of the 1969 nautical chart 5534 (*Suisun Bay*) lies over the first crime scene. | About 1 in 8; weak |
| The best-known Z32 candidate, "IN THREE AND THREE EIGHTHS RADIANS TEN", is one of **20 of 64** distances on the same line that pass the cipher's only test. The cipher cannot confirm it. | Measured |
| The wettest day of the 1969–70 season at Vallejo was **20 Dec 1969**. That was the first anniversary of the Lake Herman Road murders and the date of his "drownding" letter, and he later blamed the rain for his "dud" bomb. | Factual coincidence |

## What it proves, and what it does not

- ✅ **It shows** that his own instructions (magnetic north, radians, inches) do not scatter across the Bay Area. They gather on **one road, Lake Herman Road**, where his killing began.
- ✅ **It shows** why Z32 can't be solved from the symbols alone. There are only three repeated symbols, and thousands of English texts fit them.
- ❌ **It does not** decrypt Z32. No claimed plaintext, including the ones discussed, is confirmed.
- ❌ **It does not** identify the Zodiac. The suspect chapter only summarises the public record.
- ❌ **It does not** show that a bomb existed. None was ever found, and a bluff aimed at a meaningful place is the likeliest reading.

Every claim in the report is labelled **FACT**, **MEASURED**, **INFERENCE** or **WEAK**. The report also separates *an answer* from *a plausible explanation* and from *independent confirmation*.

## Verdict

| Question | Best answer | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Where does the code point? | The Lake Herman Road corridor; best single point 38.0950 N, 122.1440 W | Corridor about 4 in 10; the point about 1 in 5 |
| Where would a real device fit? | West shoulder of Columbus Parkway, just north of Lake Herman Road | Low |
| Was there a bomb? | Probably not | About 9 in 10 that nothing was there |
| What does Z32 say? | Not deciphered | Under 1 in 10 for any single text |
| Suspect | Arthur Leigh Allen is the only police-named suspect on the record; all physical comparisons were negative | No identification |

## Respect

Real people were killed at the places named here. Nothing in this work is a reason to visit, dig at or disturb any site. Anything worth checking should go to the authorities.
